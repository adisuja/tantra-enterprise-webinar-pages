Who We Are

GrowthClub Marketing LLP ("GrowthClub", "we", "us") runs this website and the Tantra live sessions you register for here. We are an omni-channel AI RevOps platform.

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or register for a session, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

The Information We Collect

  • Contact details you give us: your first name, last name, email address and phone number with its country code.
  • Session details: the session you registered for, whether you attended and for how long, and any questions you send in the session chat.
  • Campaign details: the ad or link that brought you here, read from the tags on the address you arrived at, and your time zone, read from your browser so reminders reach you at a sensible hour.
  • Technical details: your IP address, browser and device, which our security provider checks to keep bots out of the form.
  • Payment details: if you buy from us, Dodo Payments processes the payment. We see the amount and the status of the payment, never your full card number.

How We Collect It

You give us most of it directly when you fill in the registration form, attend a session, book a call or reply to our messages. The rest arrives automatically from your browser while you use this website, and from the platforms that run our sessions, payments and ads.

How We Use It

  1. To register you for the session and send your personal join link.
  2. To remind you before the session starts, by email, WhatsApp, text message or a short phone call, some of which an AI voice assistant makes for us.
  3. To follow up after the session with the replay, resources and offers related to what you came to learn.
  4. To send marketing emails you can stop at any time with the unsubscribe link in each one.
  5. To measure which ads and channels bring people to our sessions, and to show our ads on LinkedIn and Meta to people who have visited.
  6. To improve our sessions, this website and our services.
  7. To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They let a site remember you between pages and visits, so you do not have to tell it the same things twice.

The types we may use

  • Essential: needed for the website to work and stay secure, for example the check that keeps bots out of the registration form.
  • Analytics: count visits and show which pages people use, so we can improve them.
  • Functional: remember choices such as your country code, and your first name on the page after you register.
  • Advertising: set by ad platforms such as LinkedIn and Meta to measure our ads and show them to people who have visited.

Your choices

Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies. If you block essential ones, parts of this website, including the registration form, may stop working.

Cookies set by other companies

Some cookies come from the third-party services that appear on our pages. Their own cookie policies govern how they use them, and we do not control them.

Updates

When we change how we use cookies, we update this section and the date at the top of the page.

How We Protect It

Every page and form on this website travels over an encrypted connection. Cloudflare screens the registration form for bots and abuse. Inside our team, only the people who need your information to serve you can see it.

No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn that your information has been exposed, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your information while you are on our list, attend our sessions or work with us, and for as long afterwards as our legal and accounting obligations require. Ask us to delete it and we will, apart from the records the law makes us keep.

Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it or stop using it for marketing. Email team@growthclubpro.com and we will act on your request.

To stop messages straight away, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or reply STOP to any WhatsApp or text message.

Anti-Spam and Unsolicited Messaging Policy

GrowthClub Marketing LLP does not allow anyone to advertise, promote or link to this website, usetantra.com or any of their subdomains through unsolicited messages. That covers bulk or commercial email, text and picture messages, automated calls, instant messages, social media direct messages, and comments on forums or blogs.

We never send to bought, rented, appended or scraped lists. Every marketing email we send names us as the sender, carries a one-click unsubscribe that we honour quickly, and goes only to people who asked to hear from us.

Our team, partners, affiliates and contractors must follow this policy, including a ban on redirect or click-tracking domains that point to our websites from unsolicited messages. Anyone who breaks it loses their access or partnership, and we may take legal action.

To report abuse, email team@growthclubpro.com. We review every report.
