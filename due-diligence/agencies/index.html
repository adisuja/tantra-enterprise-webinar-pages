Motoz Digital

AI-powered growth agency for B2B SaaS companies

  • Agencies
  • Early at the start: under $25K a month ESTIMATE
  • Webinar funnel

21 sales calls from cold

every client came by referral before

Patrick LundbomFounder & CEO, Motoz DigitalLinkedIn profile of Patrick Lundbom (opens in a new tab)

  1. 1Where they started

    Every Motoz client came by referral. There was no cold channel, so new business rose and fell with word of mouth.

  2. 2What we built

    • A complete webinar funnel: registration, reminders and post-webinar nurture for cold audiences
    • Calendar-invite outreach from 17,100 to 154,261 invites per session
    • Three landing pages tested head to head, plus email and WhatsApp reminders
    • CRM workflows, dashboards and analytics from invite to booked call

  3. 3What changed

    • 21 sales calls booked from cold audiences across 6 webinars
    • 1,092 people came live, Aug 2025 to Jan 2026
    • About $30K of cold pipeline in 3 months (pipeline modelled)
