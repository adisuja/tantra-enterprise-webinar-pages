What the Tantra Platform Does

The platform your funnel runs on during the build and for the year after.

Two campaign types
Sequences put Gmail, LinkedIn and WhatsApp steps in one ordered flow and stop for a contact when a reply is detected. Calendar campaigns send Google Calendar invitations and report every RSVP (Yes, No, Maybe).
Your own accounts
Sends from your own mailboxes and social accounts, never from a shared pool.
Five safeguards on every send
A do-not-contact check (suppression and unsubscribe), per-mailbox daily and hourly caps, a sending window in the recipient's time zone, automatic suppression on a hard bounce, one-click unsubscribe.
Warm-up between your own mailboxes
A peer exchange between your connected Workspace mailboxes: it starts at 5 emails a day per mailbox and adds 5 every 3 days.
A daily deliverability check
Reports SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX and blocklists, scored out of 100.
No open pixel, ever
Click tracking is off by default. A custom tracking domain takes one CNAME.
AI personalization on your own provider
One model call per email, billed by your own provider (your key or your ChatGPT or Claude subscription). No AI credits, no markup.
WhatsApp without the Business API
Connect an existing number, normal or Business app, by scanning a QR code.
One inbox
Email and social replies in one queue, with their campaign attached and intent classified.
Priced per company
No per-seat or per-mailbox charges.
