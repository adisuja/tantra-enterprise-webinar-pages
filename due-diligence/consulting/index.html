      Team
      220+ people
      HQ
      Hicksville, New York
      Founded
      2004
      Engagement
      4 months

      1. 1Where they started

        Neural IT sold to personal-injury law firms through referrals and cold email. Attorneys are busy, and a cold email rarely earns a call.

      2. 2What we built

        • A webinar series for PI attorneys on faster, stronger demand letters
        • Calendar-invite outreach to 10,000 to 50,000 attorneys and staff per session
        • Title experiments on every session to find the hook that fills the room
        • Attendee analysis after every session to sharpen the next

      3. 3What changed

        • Live attendance grew from 17 to 127 in 5 webinars
        • 6 sales calls booked with personal-injury firms
        • 141,734 attorneys and legal staff reached, Jan to Apr 2026
