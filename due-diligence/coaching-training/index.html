Two Programmes, Measured Week by Week

Growth Idea

A UK business-coaching group for owner-led SMBs. The funnel targeted founders and MDs of construction, contracting and trades businesses. Contact: Shweta.

The problem

A coaching programme with no funnel to scale it.

What we built

  • Calendar-invite outreach at 12,000 to 120,000 invites per webinar, plus LinkedIn event invites and email
  • Registration, and a long and a short landing page tested against each other
  • Reminders, the live session and the follow-up
  • A weekly analytics sheet and a 12-item experiment log
  • Content calendars and a leads tracker

Timeline

11 webinars, May 2025 to Feb 2026. First webinar 6 May 2025; best webinar 28 Aug 2025.

Registration rate rose from 0.23% to 2.0%. At the best webinar about 16% of registrants came live (321), and there were about 8 sales calls per 100 live (25 calls, 18 MQLs, 2 clients).

From the experiment log

  • An event description change targeted 200 registrations and got 332.
  • 100,000 calendar invites targeted 250 attendees and got 441.
  • The long landing page targeted 70 registrations and got 96.
