Growth Idea

Company Profile

A UK business-coaching group for owner-led SMBs, filling its programme through webinars.

Challenge

Its first webinar (6 May 2025) reached 12,898 people. 30 registered and 7 came live.

Results

  • 765,105 people reached across 11 webinars, May 2025 to Feb 2026.
  • Registration rate rose from 0.23% to 2.0%.
  • 80 sales calls booked, 47 MQLs and 4 clients won.
  • Reached per webinar12,898 → 99,859
  • Registered per webinar30 → 1,990
  • Came live per webinar7 → 321

First webinar (6 May 2025) to best webinar (28 Aug 2025).

FintechCircle

Company Profile

A London fintech network selling a board-readiness programme through a live episode series.

Challenge

Episode 1 (5 Mar 2026) reached 10,000 people. 59 came live and 1 sales call was booked.

Results

  • 17 sales calls booked across episodes 1 to 3 (1, then 9, then 7).
  • Outreach grew from 10,000 to 30,000 per episode, then 38,138 calendar invites for episode 4.
  • 175 programme applications, 12 Mar to 1 Oct 2026.
  • Outreach per episode10,000 → 30,000
  • Came live per episode59 → 117
  • Calls booked per episode1 → 9

Episode 1 (5 Mar 2026) to the best of episodes 2 and 3.
