Growth Idea

UK business-coaching group for owner-led SMBs

  • Coaching, Training and Education
  • Scale at the start: $100K to $500K a month ESTIMATE
  • Webinar funnel

80 sales calls booked

from 11 webinars and 765,105 people reached

ShwetaFounder & CEO, Growth Idea

  1. 1Where they started

    An award-winning coaching programme with no funnel to scale it. Its first webinar (6 May 2025) reached 12,898 people: 30 registered and 7 came live.

  2. 2What we built

    • Calendar-invite outreach at 12K to 120K invites per webinar, plus LinkedIn event invites and email
    • A long and a short landing page, tested against each other
    • Reminders, the live session, follow-up and a weekly analytics sheet
    • A 12-item growth experiment log

  3. 3What changed

    • 765,105 people reached across 11 webinars, May 2025 to Feb 2026
    • Registration rate up from 0.23% to 2.0%
    • 80 sales calls, 47 MQLs and 4 clients won

FintechCircle

London fintech network selling a board-readiness programme

  • Coaching, Training and Education
  • Growth at the start: $25K to $100K a month ESTIMATE
  • Webinar episode series

17 sales calls in 3 episodes

plus 175 programme applications

SusanneFounder & Chair, FintechCircle

  1. 1Where they started

    A live episode series for a board-readiness programme. Episode 1 (5 Mar 2026) reached 10,000 people: 59 came live and 1 sales call was booked.

  2. 2What we built

    • Calendar-first outreach, 10K to 38K invites per episode, plus email
    • A branded landing page and a LinkedIn lead form
    • Reminders, replays and a programme application form with approval review
    • A weekly sync tracker and cold email analytics

  3. 3What changed

    • 17 sales calls booked across episodes 1 to 3 (1, then 9, then 7)
    • Outreach grew from 10,000 to 30,000 per episode, then 38,138 invites for episode 4
    • 175 programme applications, 12 Mar to 1 Oct 2026
