How to use your Revenue Plan

1Read your plan in 30 seconds
12-month revenue goal$500,000Calendar invites1.1MRegistrants7,419Customers4.2
  1. The dark panel on the right (at the top on a phone) is your plan. It starts with your 12-month revenue goal.
  2. Under it, every stage of the funnel shows what you need each month to hit that goal: how many invites, emails or connection requests to send, then how many sign-ups, attendees, calls and customers that makes.
  3. Change your goal right there in the panel. Everything under it recalculates.
2Switch funnel types
Webinar98% fitBook a Call92% fitLaunch56% fit
  1. The tabs under the title hold all ten funnels, ranked for your business. The percentage is how well each one fits your answers.
  2. Click a tab and the calculator and the plan switch to that funnel. The numbers you changed on one funnel stay with it.
  3. On a phone, swipe the tabs sideways.
3Change any number
Show-Up Rate25 %↺
  1. Every box in the calculator is a number you can change: the rate for each stage, your price, and how clients buy.
  2. Type a new number. The plan on the right updates as you type.
  3. A box you changed turns amber and shows a small arrow. Click the arrow to go back to the number built from your answers.
4Set your traffic channels
Calendar invites0.6 %80 %LinkedIn0.4 %20 %
  1. Stage 1 lists the channels you picked. The first box is how well the channel converts; the second is the share of your sign-ups it brings.
  2. Change the shares to try a different mix. The plan shows how much each channel must send to reach your goal.
  3. Your own email list and followers count first, before any outreach.
5Check the maths and edit benchmarks
Show-Up Rate12 %18 %25 %
  1. How Your Plan Is Calculated, under the calculator, shows the revenue maths step by step, then every stage: the benchmark range behind each rate and each conversion with its numbers.
  2. Low, typical and strong are editable. Change them and your rate moves inside the new range; the whole plan follows.
  3. Reset benchmarks puts the ranges back to the ones built from our own webinar data.
6Save, share and start again
Copy plan linkReset my changes
  1. Copy plan link copies a link with your answers and every change. It never carries your name or email.
  2. Reset my changes puts every number back to the values built from your answers.
  3. Your plan is also saved in this browser, so it is here when you come back.
