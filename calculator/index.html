    1Read your plan in 30 seconds

    1. Start with the dark card on the right (on a phone, it is at the top). It answers three questions: how much revenue, how much traffic you need, and how many customers and calls that means each month.
    2. The KPI to hit at each stage lists every rate. Plan is the rate your numbers assume. Target is what good looks like for this funnel.
    3. What moves your numbers most ranks the three changes worth the most to you, with how to make each one happen.
    2Switch funnel types
    Webinar98% fitBook a Call92% fitLaunch56% fit
    1. The row of tabs above the settings holds all ten funnels, ranked for your business. The percentage is how well each fits your answers; the top three are outlined.
    2. Click a tab and everything switches to that funnel: the stages, every KPI and the plan on the right. Edits you made on one funnel stay with that funnel.
    3. To compare, use Your top funnels, side by side at the bottom of the right column. Click a funnel name there to open it. On a phone, swipe the tabs sideways.
    3Change any number
    Show-Up Rate50 %Auto
    1. Every white box with a % sign is a KPI you can change. Type a new rate, then press Enter or click away: the whole plan recalculates.
    2. The bar under each KPI shows where your number sits. The shaded part runs from typical to strong; the dot is your number.
    3. A box you changed turns amber and gets an Auto button. Click Auto to go back to the value built from your survey answers.
    4. Click a stage title (Traffic, Nurture and so on) to open or close it.
    4Set your goal, price and traffic
    12-month goal$500,000Plan fromMy traffic
    1. Change the 12-month revenue goal or the price at the top, and the traffic you need updates at once.
    2. Clients buy switches between the sales-call stages (booking, show-up, close) and the buy-online stages. Funnels that always end in a call hide it.
    3. Plan from: My traffic turns the plan around. Type the visits you can drive each month, and the plan shows the revenue they make.
    5Save, share and start again
    Copy my plan linkSave as PDFReset my edits
    1. Copy my plan link copies a link with your answers and every edit. Send it to your team; it never carries your name or email.
    2. Save as PDF opens every stage and prints the plan.
    3. Reset my edits clears the numbers you typed on every funnel and keeps your survey answers. Your plan is also saved in this browser, so it is here when you come back.
