Video 1: Funnels. "Why Your Funnel Leaks Before Anyone Books a Call"

Runtime: about 6 minutes. Config slot: media.indoctrination_videos.funnels.

[0:00 to 0:30] Hook - Key line: "You've had people sign up and never come. Or come, nod along, and never book. That's not bad luck. That's a step in your funnel nobody owns." - Promise: in six minutes you'll see the five steps every booked call passes through, and how to find the one where you lose the most people.

[0:30 to 1:00] Who I am - One line only: founder of Tantra and GrowthClub, a decade building revenue engines for other people, 400+ brands in 21+ countries.

[1:00 to 2:00] The five steps - Draw it on screen: reach, register, attend, book, close. - Key line: "Every booked call passes through five steps. Lose people at any one of them and it doesn't matter how good the rest is." - Marketing gets judged on the first steps, sales on the last. The money leaks in the handover between them, and nobody owns the handover.

[2:00 to 3:30] Real numbers at each step - Show the Growth Idea ledger: 11 webinars, May 2025 to Feb 2026. 765,105 reached, 6,517 registered, 1,384 came live, 80 sales calls booked, 47 MQLs, 4 clients won. - Key line: "Look at where the numbers drop. That's where the work is." - Walk the drops: reach to registration is the widest gap, so the invite and the page matter most at the start.

[3:30 to 5:00] First webinar against best webinar - First (6 May 2025): 12,898 reached, 30 registered, 7 live. Best (28 Aug 2025): 99,859 reached, 1,990 registered, 321 live, 25 sales calls. - Two things grew: reach, and the registration rate, from 0.23% to 2.0%. - The experiment log shows the kind of changes they tested: an event description change targeted 200 registrations and got 332; the long landing page targeted 70 and got 96. Say that the log is indicative, not a controlled test. - Key line: "Change one thing a week, give it a target before it runs, and write down what happened."

[5:00 to 5:45] Pick your conversion mechanism - A live event, a video sales funnel or a multi-day workshop, whichever fits your offer. - If a person on a call closes your deals, a live session is usually the place to start, because the call is the next natural step.

[5:45 to 6:15] Checklist and close - Read the four checklist lines on the page. - Key line: "Next video: the operating system that keeps this running every single week."

Proof used: PROOF_PACK sections 1 and 3 (Growth Idea measured totals, first and best webinar, derived rates, experiment log marked indicative).

Video 2: RevOps OS. "The Weekly Operating System That Keeps the Funnel Running"

Runtime: about 7 minutes. Config slot: media.indoctrination_videos.revops_os.

[0:00 to 0:30] Hook - Key line: "Most funnels don't fail on launch day. They fail weeks later, when nobody looks at the numbers and the follow-up quietly stops." - Promise: the four parts of the weekly rhythm we run with every client.

You have a list." - Every stage has one owner and one next action.

[2:00 to 3:00] Part 2: Speed after a booking - Our rule: a person from the team calls every enquiry within five minutes in working hours (our sales playbook). - Missed calls: decide your rule in advance and say it in the booking confirmation. Ours: a missed evaluation call is not rescheduled. - Key line: "Decide what happens after an enquiry before the first one comes in."

If it's not on the sheet, it didn't happen." - Read it every Monday. Find the biggest drop. That's the week's job.

[4:30 to 5:45] Part 4: The experiment log - Columns: what you change, the target, the result, the decision. - Example from Growth Idea's 12-item log: 100,000 calendar invites targeted 250 attendees and got 441. Say it was one of 12 and the log is indicative. - Key line: "Every experiment gets a target before it runs. Otherwise you'll call everything a win."

[5:45 to 6:40] Where Tantra fits - Sequences put Gmail, LinkedIn and WhatsApp steps in one ordered flow and stop for a contact when a reply is detected. - One inbox: email and social replies in one queue, with their campaign attached and intent classified. - Say "when a reply is detected", never "instantly".

[6:40 to 7:00] Checklist and close - Read the four checklist lines. - Key line: "Next video: where the people come from."
